How a session works

Hit. Swap.
Compare. Decide.

Most players never get to feel two setups side by side. In a session, that’s the whole point — and you walk away strung.

Poly testingTension testingString typesFresh stringing
EducationExperimentationImmediate feedback

Hitting also available — upon request

  1. The lineup

    I show up with racquets pre-strung in different setups — polys, string types and tensions — arranged for side-by-side testing.

  2. You test

    Hit with each one and feel the difference in spin, control, power and comfort, back to back.

  3. I string on site

    While you test, I keep stringing more racquets based on what you’re liking — narrowing in on your setup as we go.

  4. Leave strung

    The session ends with a fresh stringing on your own racquet, in your new setup.
